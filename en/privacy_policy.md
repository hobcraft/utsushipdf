# Utsushi PDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Utsushi PDF (“the app”) collects no personal information.

## Information collected

The app never sends any information off your Mac — no identifying information, no usage
statistics, no crash data. It contains no analytics and no advertising SDK. There is no
account to create. The app has no networking feature at all.

If you have allowed “Share with App Developers” in macOS system settings, crash reports
may be shared with the developer through Apple when the app crashes. That sharing is a
macOS feature, not something the app does — you can change it under System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

## What stays on your Mac

The app reads the files you drag and drop, and writes the resulting PDFs, entirely on
your Mac. None of this leaves your device, and the developer cannot see any of it.

## Permission to control other apps

To convert Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Keynote and Numbers files, the app operates
those applications directly. The first time you convert a given file type, macOS asks
you to allow the app to control that application. This permission is required for the
conversion itself — it does not send your file contents anywhere.

## Sharing with third parties

There is nothing to share, since the app sends no information anywhere.
